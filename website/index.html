AI Assistant

AI assistance, right where your team works.

Connect your existing AI services to bring real-time meeting assistance into Honco Chat. The assistant panel opens next to the meeting, and its results stay with the meeting afterwards.

Honco Chat provides the assistant panel, the meeting association, the secure server-side integration boundary and the notifications. Real-time analysis is produced by the AI service you connect to it.

Security

Your workspace. Your infrastructure.

Honco Chat is deployed on servers you control. Conversations, files, recordings and meeting insights are stored in your own database, and integrations are wired up server-side so credentials never reach the browser.

  • Self-hosted deployment

    Runs on your own servers. No vendor cloud in the path of your team's data.

  • PostgreSQL-backed

    All application data is kept in a PostgreSQL database that you operate and back up.

  • Authenticated access

    Every workspace request is authenticated. Nothing is served to anonymous visitors.

  • Roles and permissions

    Administrative functions are limited to administrators; members see what their role allows.

  • Team and channel authorization

    Meetings, tasks, recordings and support requests are visible only to members of the team and channel they belong to.

  • Server-side secrets

    Integration credentials stay on the server. The browser never receives a service token.

Also in place: request rate limiting, security response headers and input validation on the workspace APIs. Honco Chat does not currently claim any third-party compliance certification.
